About Smart Civic

Our mission is to bridge the gap between citizens and government departments, ensuring quick and transparent resolution of civic issues through modern technology.

For Citizens

Report problems easily, attach photos, pinpoint exact locations on interactive maps, and track progress until resolution.

For Departments

Receive auto-assigned complaints, manage workflow intelligently, update statuses efficiently, and never miss an SLA.

For Admins

Monitor overall performance, analyze data through beautiful interactive dashboards, and ensure civic accountability.
