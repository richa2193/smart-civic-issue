How CivicSaathi Enforces Accountability

A completely transparent, step-by-step workflow designed to ensure every municipal issue is tracked, automatically routed, and fully resolved.

Step 1

Report & Evidence

Citizens submit geolocated reports with photographic evidence and detailed descriptions directly from the field.

Step 2

Algorithmic Routing

Our categorization engine instantly parses the issue and assigns it precisely to the correct municipal department.

Step 3

Department Review

A designated department officer acknowledges the assignment and begins preliminary assessment of the reported site.

Step 4

SLA Enforcement

The complaint is strictly tracked against the department's Service Level Agreement deadline, automatically flagging if overdue.

Step 5

Community Upvotes

Fellow citizens can independently upvote existing grievances on the Public Ledger to elevate high-priority community issues.

Step 6

Real-Time Updates

The reporting citizen is actively notified via automated emails and in-app alerts whenever the grievance status formally changes.

Step 7

Formal Resolution

The department officer officially marks the issue as resolved, attaching closing remarks and visual proof of completion.

Step 8

Citizen Verification

The resolution is verified by the citizen, and these metrics instantly feed the municipal dashboard for public transparency.
