Standard Operating Procedure

The official 8-step protocol ensuring transparency and accountability for every grievance filed.

1

Report

Submit verified details and photographic evidence of the civic issue.

2

Pin Location

Provide exact GPS coordinates to ensure accurate mapping and dispatch.

3

Auto-Routing

The system algorithmically directs your grievance to the correct municipal department.

4

Dept. Review

Departmental officers assess the issue and assign a field team.

5

SLA Tracking

Strict Service Level Agreement timelines are enforced to prevent delays.

6

Community Upvote

Citizens can collectively prioritize pressing issues through the public ledger.

7

Resolution

The assigned department fixes the problem and uploads official proof of work.

8

Citizen Verification

You independently verify the fix and close the feedback loop publicly.
